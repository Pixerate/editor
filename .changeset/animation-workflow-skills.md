Two new animation workflow skills, each with evals: `pixerate-animation-motion-graphics` (one idea per shot, staggered entrances, natural easing, holds, fitting a length) and `pixerate-animation-logo-reveal` (a short reveal from the logo's parts, on the beat, ending on the held logo). Skill evals can now start from an animation scene (`AnimationEvalFixture`).
