Lens objects in the vector editor: a closed path that shows what is drawn below it magnified, fish-eyed, recoloured (brighten, invert, tinted grayscale, colour add, colour limit, heat map), as wireframe or through a see-through fill, like CorelDRAW's Lens. The lens follows edits below it and exports to SVG, PDF and Lottie. New `setLens`, `vector.setLens` / `vector.removeLens` and `<VectorLensPanel>` in React and Svelte.
