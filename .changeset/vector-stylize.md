Bitmap-to-vector stylise in the vector editor: turn a PNG into mosaic tiles, halftone dots, low-poly triangles or engraving lines (`stylizeImage`, `stylizeVectorImage` and `vector.mosaicImage` / `halftoneImage` / `lowPolyImage` / `hatchImage`); the trace panels offer the styles.
