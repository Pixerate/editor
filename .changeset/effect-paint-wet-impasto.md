Paint editor: effect paint now works with wet and impasto brushes, and with sticker sprays. Watercolour, gouache and oil brushes take each pixel's colour from the effect, shifted by any paint the brush picks up; the pattern then flows, blooms and granulates with the water, and replays stay exact. Impasto brushes take the effect's colours and keep the same relief as a flat colour. Each sprayed sticker takes the effect's colour at its centre and stays recolourable; none lands where the effect is transparent. These brushes no longer refuse effect paint.
