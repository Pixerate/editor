Add ghost paint and invisible ink (`ghost` facet with `linger` and `smear`): wet paint that flows and mixes visibly with the colours it meets while wet, then fades away completely without leaving a trace of its own colour, leaving only the smear where it lifted and dragged the paint already on the layer. Includes presets `ghost-wash`, `ghost-gouache`, `ghost-oil` and `invisible-ink`, `ghost.remaining` per layer in `get_wet_paint`, Linger and Smear sliders in React, Svelte and Swift brush panels, and full Swift parity.
