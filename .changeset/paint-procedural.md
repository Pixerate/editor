Procedural brushes, bristles, stickers and stencils in the paint editor. Particle brushes (`engine: 'particle'`: spring-and-mass strings, springs, orbits and flow particles, drawn as dots, lines or bead sequences), bristle brushes that splay, flatten, bend and run dry bristle by bristle, and sticker spray of procedural stamps that stay editable objects on a sticker layer (`list_stickers`, `update_sticker`, `arrange_stickers`, `remove_stickers`). Stencils: ruler and arc guides that strokes follow, and line and shape masks (`add_stencil`, `update_stencil`, `remove_stencils`), recorded with strokes so replays keep them. `@pixerate/editor/paint` adds `paintPathStroke` / `pathStrokeDabs` for brushstrokes along polyline paths with pressure and tilt profiles. React and Svelte add particle, bristle and stamp controls, stencil buttons and outlines, and `<StickerPanel>`.
