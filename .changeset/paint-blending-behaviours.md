Paint editor phase 2 blending and painterly mixing. `@pixerate/editor/paint` gains the shared pick-up / mix exchange (`PaintExchange`, `PaintMedium`, `TileMedium`, `exchangeRates`), which the Mixer Brush now runs on with identical output and which the wet layer will plug into as another medium; a blender (`blenderDab`) that mixes the colours under and near the tip through the pigment model, with strength and blend distance; and a patch-carrying smudge (`SmudgeTip`) with strength, length and an RGB or pigment mixing option. The paint editor adds `blender` and `smudge` brush engines and drives them, the Mixer Brush and wet-capable dab brushes through a new dab-by-dab engine (`PaintDabStroke`) with full pen input, paper grain and symmetry. Brushes gain `behavior`: pick-up and mix ratios, a dirty brush that carries the last colour it touched into the next stroke, and a multi-coloured brush (colours across the tip, or per dab in sequence or at random). New presets: Pigment smudge, Dirty round, Multi-colour flat and Palette dabber; Blender and Smudge use the new engines. Recorded strokes keep their starting load so pick-up strokes replay exactly. The mixing pad mixes measured parts (`mixOnPad`), and pads can be kept by name and are saved with the document. New agent tools `mix_on_pad`, `save_mixing_pad`, `list_mixing_pads`, `load_mixing_pad` and `remove_mixing_pad`; `set_brush` takes `behavior`, `blender` and `smudge`; `mix_colors` takes `model` (`pigment` or `rgb`).
