Distortion brushes in the vector editor: a Distort Brush tool (D) that smudges, twirls, attracts, repels or roughens the outlines under a soft round brush, like CorelDRAW's distortion tools, with `vector.smudgeBrush` / `twirlBrush` / `attractBrush` / `repelBrush` / `roughenBrush` for agents and the brush options and circle in both bindings.
