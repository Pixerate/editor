Paint editor painting guides and colour tools. Mirror and kaleidoscope painting (vertical, horizontal, diagonal and dual mirrors, kaleidoscopes of 2 to 32 segments) paints every copy as one stroke and one undo step. Perspective guides with one to three vanishing points snap pen strokes to their lines. A tracing image sits under the paint in the view only and can supply stroke colours, and floating reference images let you pick colours. Colour harmonies work on the painter's red-yellow-blue wheel or the RGB wheel, swatches are saved with the document, and a mixing pad is painted with a pigment Mixer Brush and smeared with a palette knife. Adds 19 agent tools (`set_symmetry`, `set_perspective_guide`, `set_tracing_image`, `add_reference_image`, `color_harmony`, `mixing_pad_stroke`, …) and `snapToPerspective` on `paint_stroke`.
