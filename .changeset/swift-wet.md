The Swift paint editor paints wet media (watercolour, gouache, wet oils and the wet tools on the TypeScript wet kernel's maths, bit-exact), thick paint lit at render time, light paint with rim, clarity and spill, and metallic and backlit paper. Agents get the wet, thick and light tools; `PaintEditorView` gets wet, light and spill panels and ticks the simulation. All 23 paint conformance scenarios pass.
