Brushstrokes on vector paths: a `brushstroke` stroke layer paints a path with a natural-media brush from the paint editor's library through the shared brushstroke renderer, with a size and a pressure profile; drawn on both canvases and exported to SVG (images, data round-trips) and PDF (image objects). New `addBrushstroke`, `setBrushstroke`, `vector.listBrushes` / `addBrushstroke` / `setBrushstroke`, and brushstroke rows in the appearance panels.
