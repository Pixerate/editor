Three new vector workflow skills, each with evals: `pixerate-vector-icon-set` (one grid, padding, stroke, caps and radius across a set, crisp coordinates, symbols, SVG), `pixerate-vector-logo` (marks from live booleans, optical corrections, an aligned lockup, colour variants as symbols, outlined delivery files) and `pixerate-vector-illustration` (block in back to front, gradients and stacked fills, masks, blends, subtle shadows). `pixerate-vector` is back under 450 lines, with its drawing best practices in `references/best-practices.md`. Skill evals can now start from vector artwork and check with read-only commands such as `vector.list`.
